## 2026 homepage schedule

- `index.html` is the L-29 training homepage approved by Burhan on September 30, 2026. It replaces the race-week homepage; see HOMEPAGE-TRAINING-IMPLEMENTATION.md.
- `racing.html` and `racing/index.html` preserve Team Zia's race coverage, gallery, films and downloads.
- `home-full.html` preserves the earlier LB AeroFlight business homepage as historical content; it is not the current training design.
- `open-house-2026.html` preserves the completed August 29 Open House campaign as an unindexed archive.
- `rsvp.html` is intentionally closed and directs visitors to the current Team Zia campaign.

Do not delete the preserved pages when changing campaigns. Swap the homepage only after confirming the next publishing phase.
